## 01 · Cover

Imagine this: you have just defended your graduation project. That evening, you are sitting with friends, drinking beer and talking about how it went. You want to post a photo from the night, then remember that your supervisor follows you.
Would you post it?

## 02 · The problem

From the same day, you also have a photo with your grandmother, who came to the graduation ceremony. You want to share that moment with your family. Your colleagues should see the graduation project and what you built.
One day, but each post has its own audience. When those audiences mix, you choose the safest photo or post nothing at all.

## 03 · The rings

That is why I am building selecont: a social platform with separate contexts inside one account. The name comes from select context.

## 04–07 · The disc

Meet Grisha. His family sees photos with loved ones. His friends see the celebration after graduation. His colleagues see the graduation project. Each circle can have its own profile photo and bio. The author decides who sees what.

*Changes: 04 — “Meet Grisha. His family…”; 05 — “His friends…”; 06 — “His colleagues…”; 07 — “Each circle…”. Do not read this note aloud.*

## 08 · Euler circles

Grisha wants to share the diploma photo with all three circles.
At the center is one post. Each circle is a selected audience. Grisha publishes once, and his family, friends and colleagues all see it.
But should the discussion under that post be shared too?

## 09 · Comments

His mother writes, “I remember taking you to your first day of school.” His friend asks, “Will you post the party photos too?”
In selecont, those comments stay in separate threads. His family sees its discussion, and his friends see theirs.
The context connects the profile, access to the post and the comments below it.

## 10 · Working prototype

This already works in the local prototype. You can create circles, publish one post to several audiences, keep discussions separate and revoke access. The screen shows the real interface with test accounts.

## 12 · New follower suggestion — future feature
